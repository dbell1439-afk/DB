# ✉️ 7-Email Nurture Sequence

Copy-and-paste starting drafts for your email provider (ConvertKit, Beehiiv,
MailerLite, etc.). Trigger when someone subscribes via the free checklist
(`tag: lm-readiness`) or any lead magnet.

**Compliance for every email:** include a footer line —
> *Educational information only. Not legal, financial, medical, tax, or
> government benefit advice. Not affiliated with OPM, SSA, the VA, or any
> government agency. Unsubscribe anytime.*

Spacing suggestion: Email 1 immediately, then Days 2, 4, 6, 9, 12, 15.

**Preview:** The first-year vs. second-year math.

> - **First 12 months:** 60% of high-3 − 100% of SSDI

**Subject:** Don't drop FEHB before you retire (the 5-year rule)
**Preview:** And the FEGLI election people regret.

> - **FEHB:** generally you must be enrolled for the 5 years before retirement to
>   keep it as a retiree. Confirm your eligibility *before* changing plans.
> - **FEGLI:** the 75% / 50% / no-reduction choice has very different costs after
>   65. Model later years, not just the monthly premium.
