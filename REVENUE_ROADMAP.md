# 💰 30-Day Revenue Roadmap

A low-cost, fast-to-revenue plan. Priority order of monetization:

1. **Email capture** with free lead magnets (build the asset)
2. **Low-ticket digital products** ($9–$49 toolkits)
3. **Affiliate offers** (educational comparison resources)
4. **Consultation intake** (paid educational strategy sessions)
5. **Referral / lead-gen** relationships with licensed professionals

> Reminder: stay an education brand. No "best" product claims, no advice, no
> implied government affiliation. Every monetization step keeps the disclaimers.

---

## Week 1 — Publish the MVP & turn on capture

**Goal:** a live site that captures emails and can take its first sale.

- [ ] Deploy the MVP to Vercel with a custom domain.
- [ ] Create and connect the **free OPM Retirement Readiness Checklist** lead
      magnet; wire `EmailSignup` to your provider.
- [ ] Launch **3 paid toolkits** first (start with the strongest intent):
      FERS Disability Prep Toolkit ($29), OPM Delay Escalation Tracker ($17),
      SSDI Offset Worksheet Pack ($19). Add real Gumroad/Payhip/Stripe links.
- [ ] Publish the **affiliate resources page** with your first real affiliate
      links (apply to programs now — approvals take days).
- [ ] Set up email capture + automated delivery of the free checklist.
- [ ] Install analytics (GA4 or Plausible) + Microsoft Clarity for heatmaps.

**Revenue levers live:** email list, 3 products, affiliate page.

## Week 2 — Content & traffic engine

**Goal:** start ranking and driving referral traffic; begin the newsletter.

- [ ] Publish **6 high-intent articles** (already drafted in `data/articles.ts`
      — verify facts, then promote). Prioritize:
  1. How the FERS Disability Retirement SSDI Offset Works
  2. Why OPM Disability Retirement Requires an SSDI Application
  3. OPM Retirement Delays: What to Track While You Wait
  4. FEHB in Retirement: Questions to Ask Before You Separate
  5. FEGLI at Retirement: 75/50/No Reduction
  6. OPM Interim Pay: Why It May Be Lower Than Expected
- [ ] Create **Pinterest pins** for each article + each free checklist (federal
      benefits audiences over-index on Pinterest for checklists/printables).
- [ ] Draft **YouTube Shorts scripts** (see ideas below).
- [ ] **Start the newsletter** — send issue #1 to your new list.

## Week 3 — Tools, bundle & intake

**Goal:** raise average order value and open higher-ticket revenue.

- [ ] Promote the **3 calculators** (already built) as link-magnets and pin them.
- [ ] Launch a **paid bundle** — Veteran Federal Employee Benefits Bundle ($49)
      and/or Medical Separation Timeline Builder ($39).
- [ ] Turn on the **consultation intake** and set a price for educational
      strategy sessions. Add a calendar link after a qualified request.
- [ ] **Outreach to licensed professionals** (disability retirement attorneys,
      CFPs, tax pros, insurance brokers) for compliant referral relationships.

## Week 4 — Optimize & retarget

**Goal:** lift conversion and start compounding.

- [ ] Publish **comparison guides** (e.g., disability vs. regular retirement;
      FEHB plan-type comparisons) — high commercial intent.
- [ ] **Optimize CTAs** using Clarity/heatmap data (test headline + button copy).
- [ ] Add **testimonials only if real** (with permission; no fabricated proof).
- [ ] Add **retargeting pixels** (Meta/Pinterest) — disclose in privacy policy.
- [ ] Improve the **email nurture sequence** (see `EMAIL_SEQUENCE.md`); add a
      product offer and a consultation invitation.

## Notes on compliant lead-gen referrals

- Only refer to **appropriately licensed** professionals, and only where
  referral compensation is permitted by that profession's rules and your state.
- Disclose any referral compensation. Never imply guaranteed outcomes.
- Keep your role as **education + introduction**, not advice or representation.
